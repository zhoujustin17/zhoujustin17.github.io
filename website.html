Filtered Momentum

Python · written 2025, rebuilt 2026

A rebuild of a momentum strategy my team wrote up in 2025, which reported a Sharpe ratio of 1.37. Reconstructing it with point-in-time S&P 500 membership, next-day execution, and transaction costs gave 0.55 instead. The gap is survivorship bias, entering at the signal's own close, and parameters chosen with the whole sample visible.
